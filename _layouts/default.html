Takahiro Maeda (前田 孝泰)

I am a machine learning researcher at the Collaborative AI Laboratory, AI Digital R&D Center, Corporate Laboratory, Toshiba Corporation, where I develop enterprise vision-language models (VLMs) and AI agents. I completed the coursework of the doctoral program without a degree (単位取得満期退学) at Toyota Technological Institute (TTI) in 2025, advised by Prof. Norimichi Ukita. I also earned my M.Eng. in electronics and information engineering at TTI in 2021 and B.Eng. in 2019 while working with Norimichi Ukita. During my studies, I was a research intern at the Robotics Institute, Carnegie Mellon University (2024) and the Frontier Research Center, Toyota Motor Corporation (2023), and a visiting student at TTI-Chicago (2019).

My research interests include computer vision, robotics, human motion prediction, and multimodal models, with a long-term interest in robust perception for intelligent assistive robots. I got a Certified Care Worker (介護福祉士) national qualification license on April, 2022.
